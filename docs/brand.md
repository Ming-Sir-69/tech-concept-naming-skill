# Essonym

Essonym is an AI-led naming companion for AI tools, agents and open-source projects. It turns a creator's intent into names with meaning and an individual identity, with English and Chinese conceived independently when useful.

Essonym 为 AI 工具、Agent 与开源项目原创命名：把创造者赋予作品的思想与气质，化为有来历、有个性的名字。

## Name

`Ess` is a clipped inspiration from English *essence*; `-onym` relates to names. Essonym is a new coinage, not an attested ancient Greek word. Suggested pronunciation: **ESS-uh-nim**.

Sources: [American Heritage: essence](https://www.ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=essence), [American Heritage: -onym](https://ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=-onym). These sources explain the components, not the newly coined complete name. Availability and trademark registration have not been established by this description.

## Selected mark

<img src="../assets/essonym-mark.png" width="240" alt="Essonym plum mark">

The selected graphic uses a compact, near-square silhouette, a flowing negative space and a retained lower-left notch. The approved color treatment is **G1: a subtle vertical plum transition**. The design theme is “let creation leave its own mark”; this is the intended expression, not a claim that every viewer will infer it.

The supplied `assets/essonym-mark.png` is the approved AI-assisted raster artwork: 1254 × 1254, RGBA with transparency. It is not an editable vector master. The nominal base/accent plum is `#785064`; the generation targets for the subtle transition were `#80586C` to `#714B60`. The delivered PNG is the visual reference, rather than a guarantee that every pixel exactly matches those targets. Preserve its silhouette and lower-left notch when reproducing it.
